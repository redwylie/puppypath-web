Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all apps published under the PuppyPath umbrella (puppypath.org), operated by Mark Wylie ("PuppyPath", "we", "us") — including PuppyPath: Day and any other puppy-care apps we release. It explains what data our apps handle, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Our approach is private by default: your data lives on your device, your photos never leave it, and we run no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.

Not every app offers every feature described below. Where a section refers to accounts, cloud sync, or sharing, it applies to the apps that provide those features — some of our apps are fully on-device with no account at all.

The short version

  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics. Our apps contain no third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or behavioral-tracking software. We do not profile you or sell your data — ever.
  • Your photos stay on your device. Puppy profile photos and the photos you attach to moments are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or shared with other people.
  • On-device data is encrypted on iOS, Android, and macOS (AES-256), with the key held in your device's secure storage (Apple Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • Cloud sync, where an app offers it, is used to back up and sync your puppy log across your devices and to the people you explicitly invite. This data is stored on our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Household notifications are optional. In apps that support sharing, you can choose to receive a notification when another member logs a meal, gives a medication dose, or starts a walk. You can turn this off at any time, and it respects your device's notification settings.
  • You can delete your account and data from within the app at any time.

What data we handle

Depending on which app you use and the features you enable, we may handle the following.

1. Information you create in the app

  • Puppy profile: name, birthday or estimated age, breed/mascot selection, and an optional profile photo.
  • Activity log: the things you record — naps and sleep, meals, potty breaks (and whether they were accidents), play, walks, training, custom activity labels, and any notes you add.
  • Health information: health-related moments and symptoms you log, medication and care reminders, their schedules, and the doses you mark as given.
  • Moment photos: photos you optionally attach to a logged moment.

2. Account information

Apps that offer cloud sync use an account to sync and back up your data. Depending on how you sign in, we may hold:

  • A unique account identifier (a random ID assigned by our authentication provider).
  • Your email address, if you sign up with email, a one-time email code, or convert a guest account. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address.
  • The sign-in method you used (email, Google, Apple, or guest).
  • A device notification token (a random delivery identifier issued by Apple or Google) if you turn on household notifications, so the relevant notifications can be delivered to your device. You can turn this off per device in Settings, which removes the token from our servers.

Where supported, you can also tap "Continue as guest" to use the app without providing any email or personal identifier. A guest account is an anonymous account; you can later save it to an email account, keeping all your data.

3. Device permissions

Our apps ask for permissions only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Camera / Photo library — only when you choose to add a photo to a puppy profile or a moment. Photos you select stay on your device.
  • Notifications — to deliver the reminders you set up (such as potty or medication reminders), which are scheduled locally on your device; and, in apps that support sharing, if you turn the feature on, to deliver household notifications when another member logs a meal, gives a medication dose, or starts a walk. Household notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's push services (see "Push notification delivery" below) and can be turned off per device in Settings.

Our apps do not request location, contacts, calendar, microphone, Bluetooth, or any other sensitive permission.

Where your data is stored

On your device

Our apps are local-first: your full puppy log is stored on your device so it works offline.

  • On iOS, Android, and macOS, this on-device database is encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The encryption key is generated on your device and stored in the platform's secure storage (Apple Keychain or Android Keystore).
  • On the web version, the browser's local storage is used. For technical reasons the web database is not encrypted at rest; we recommend the mobile apps if local encryption matters to you.

In the cloud (Supabase)

For apps that offer cloud sync, to back up your log and sync it across your devices and with people you invite, the app sends a copy of your puppy log to our backend provider, Supabase (supabase.com), which hosts our database and handles authentication on our behalf. Supabase acts as our data processor.

What is synced to the cloud: puppy profiles (name, birthday, mascot), activity sessions, logged moments, schedules, custom activity labels and tags, and medication reminders and doses.

What is never synced: your photos (puppy profile photos and moment photos) remain on your device only. Your in-app preferences (theme, display settings) also stay local.

Push notification delivery

If you turn on household notifications for a shared dog, we use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications, and on iOS these are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service (APNs). To do this, we store a device notification token on our backend, and when a member logs a relevant activity we send the notification's content — the name or email of the member who logged it, the dog's name, and the activity (for example, "Alex fed Rex") — to these services so they can deliver it to the other members' devices. We use these services only to deliver notifications; they are not used for analytics, tracking, or advertising.

Sharing your data with other people

In apps that support sharing, it is entirely opt-in. If you choose to share a dog with members of your household, you create a time-limited invite link that another person redeems to join.

  • People you invite can view and edit the shared puppy's log (the synced data listed above).
  • They cannot see your photos, because photos are never uploaded.
  • They can see the email addresses of other members of that shared dog, so everyone knows who has access.
  • If members turn on household notifications, logging a meal, giving a medication dose, or starting a walk sends a push notification to the other members' devices showing who logged it, the dog's name, and the activity. Each member controls this for their own devices and can turn it off.
  • As the owner you can remove members, hand off ownership, or a member can leave at any time. Guest (anonymous) accounts cannot create or join shared dogs.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

What we do not do

  • We do not embed any third-party analytics, telemetry, or usage-tracking software in our apps. (We may view aggregate, anonymized statistics from the App Store, such as total downloads and active devices — never information that identifies you.)
  • We do not use crash-reporting or error-tracking services.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not build advertising or marketing profiles about you.

Data retention and deletion

  • Your data stays available for as long as your account exists (or, in fully local apps, for as long as the app is installed).
  • You can delete individual entries, photos, or an entire dog from within the app.
  • In apps with an account, you can delete your account from the app's Settings. Deleting your account removes your account and the dogs you solely own from our servers, and clears the synced copy from your device. (If you co-own a shared dog, you'll be asked to hand off ownership first so the other members don't lose the shared log.)
  • When you turn off household notifications or sign out, the device notification token for that device is removed from our servers.
  • Inactive guest (anonymous) accounts that are never saved to an email may be automatically cleaned up after a period of inactivity.

Children's privacy

Our apps are tools for tracking the care of a dog and are intended for use by adults. They are not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Because our apps are local-first, you already hold a copy of your data on your device, and you can delete your data (and, in apps with an account, your account and its cloud copy) directly in the app. For any further request, contact us at the address below.
