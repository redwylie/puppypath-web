Common questions

How do I get started?

Open the app and add your dog: just a name and a birthday or estimated age. That's it; you're ready to start logging. You can add a profile photo and fine-tune details any time.

How do I log naps, meals, potty, or training?

From the main screen, tap the activity you want to record. Most things are a single tap in the moment; you can add notes or adjust the time afterward. Everything you log builds up into a clear daily timeline you can swipe through day by day.

Do I need an account?

Apps that offer cloud backup and cross-device sync use an account. You can sign in with email (a one-time code), Google, or Sign in with Apple. Your data is stored on your device first and works offline, so the app is usable even before everything finishes syncing.

How do I sync across my devices, or share with my household?

Once you're signed in, your log backs up and syncs automatically. To share a dog with family or housemates, create an invite link from the app's sharing settings; the people you invite can view and edit that dog's log. You stay in control: you can remove members or stop sharing at any time. (Your photos are never uploaded, so they're never shared.)

How do reminders work?

You can set reminders, such as a friendly "time to take the dog out" nudge or medication reminders, and the app will notify you on your schedule. Make sure notifications are enabled for the app in your device's settings. You can mute or change reminders any time.

Is my data private?

Yes. Our apps are private by default: no ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking. On iOS, Android, and macOS your on-device data is encrypted, and your photos never leave your device. The full details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data or my account?

You can delete individual entries, photos, or an entire dog from within the app. In apps with an account, you can delete your account (and its cloud copy) from the app's Settings. See the Privacy Policy for what deletion removes.

What's the difference between the app and the web version?

The mobile apps (iOS, Android) store your data encrypted on your device and offer the full experience. The web version is handy for quick viewing, but for technical reasons its local data is not encrypted at rest, so we recommend the mobile apps if local encryption matters to you.
